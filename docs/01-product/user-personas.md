# User Personas - PujaCircle

## 1. Devotee Persona: The Urban Professional

- **Name**: Aditi Sharma
- **Age**: 32 | **Location**: Bandra West, Mumbai
- **Occupation**: Senior Product Manager at a Fintech firm
- **Context**: Moved into a new 3BHK apartment with her husband and needs to organize an authentic Griha Pravesh & Vastu Shanti Puja according to North Indian traditions.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Does not know local purohits in Mumbai who speak Hindi and understand Varanasi-style rituals.
  - Busy schedule makes multiple phone calls for date fixing and samagri list coordination tedious.
  - Wants transparent expectations on ritual duration and customary dakshina without awkward negotiations.
- **Needs in PujaCircle**:
  - Fast mobile OTP login.
  - Simple address selection with accurate PIN auto-detection.
  - Filter purohits by language (Hindi) and specialization (Griha Pravesh).
  - Clear morning muhurat slot booking.
